Our Manifesto

Building a Prosperous Ghana for All

The New Patriotic Party’s vision is anchored on freedom, opportunity, and shared prosperity. Our policies are designed to create jobs, expand education, improve healthcare, and modernize Ghana’s economy.

Education

Free Senior High School and continuous investment in quality education for every Ghanaian child.

Healthcare

Strengthening the National Health Insurance Scheme and expanding access to quality healthcare.

Jobs & Economy

Supporting private sector growth, entrepreneurship, and industrialization to create sustainable jobs.

Digital Ghana

Accelerating digital transformation, e-governance, and innovation across the country.
