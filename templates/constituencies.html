Our Constituencies

Strong presence across Ghana

The New Patriotic Party has a nationwide structure that covers all 16 regions and 275 constituencies of Ghana.

From the cities to the smallest communities, our party executives, Members of Parliament, and grassroots members work tirelessly to promote development, good governance, and the welfare of the people.

We remain committed to strengthening our constituency structures so that every Ghanaian, regardless of where they live, can feel the impact of our policies and representation.
