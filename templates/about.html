About the New Patriotic Party

Development in Freedom

The New Patriotic Party (NPP) is one of Ghana’s two major political parties. It is a centre-right party that believes in liberal democracy, the rule of law, free enterprise, and individual liberty.

The party is the modern continuation of the Danquah-Busia-Dombo tradition, which has been at the forefront of Ghana’s democratic struggle since the United Gold Coast Convention (UGCC) in 1947.

Since the return to multi-party democracy in 1992, the NPP has contributed significantly to Ghana’s democratic stability and economic development. The party has produced two Presidents of the Republic:

  • H.E. John Agyekum Kufuor (2001 – 2009)
  • H.E. Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo (2017 – 2025)

Our core values remain: Democracy, Development, and Freedom. We are committed to building a prosperous, inclusive, and digitally advanced Ghana where every citizen has the opportunity to succeed.
